### S9 · FAQ

**What does an SEO audit cost?**

Audits start at $3,000. Price depends on site size, complexity, the number of markets and data costs, such as the third-party tools used to track AI answers. You'll get a fixed quote after the intro call, so there are no surprises.

**What's the difference between an SEO audit and an AI search audit?**

An SEO audit checks how search engines crawl, index and rank your site. An AI search audit checks how AI assistants mention, cite and describe your brand. Ours covers both, because many fixes help both.

**Is a GEO audit included?**

Yes. The AI answer benchmark and accuracy check are the GEO part of the audit. If AI visibility is your main concern, we can weight the audit toward it.

**How long does an audit take?**

Most audits take about four to six weeks from kickoff to readout. Larger sites, multiple markets or migration planning can add time, and we'll tell you up front.

**What do you need from us?**

Access to Google Search Console, your analytics and your CMS, plus the questions your buyers tend to ask. A 45-minute kickoff covers the rest.
